
# Strike-Through (double tilde only)

With the flag `MD_FLAG_STRIKETHROUGH_DOUBLE_ONLY`, MD4C recognizes
strike-through spans only when they are delimited with two tildes, and a
single tilde is left as literal text.

The flag is meant for input where a lone `~` is commonly used as a shorthand
for "approximately". Under the default GFM rules two such tildes in one block
pair up and strike out everything between them.

```````````````````````````````` example
Low end: ~$16/month and mid range: ~$32/month.
.
<p>Low end: <del>$16/month and mid range: </del>$32/month.</p>
.
--fstrikethrough
````````````````````````````````

With the flag, the same input keeps both tildes:

```````````````````````````````` example
Low end: ~$16/month and mid range: ~$32/month.
.
<p>Low end: ~$16/month and mid range: ~$32/month.</p>
.
--fstrikethrough --fstrikethrough-double-only
````````````````````````````````

Two tildes still delimit a strike-through span:

```````````````````````````````` example
~~Hi~~ Hello, world!
.
<p><del>Hi</del> Hello, world!</p>
.
--fstrikethrough --fstrikethrough-double-only
````````````````````````````````

A single tilde pair does not:

```````````````````````````````` example
~Hi~ Hello, world!
.
<p>~Hi~ Hello, world!</p>
.
--fstrikethrough --fstrikethrough-double-only
````````````````````````````````

Mismatched and over-long tilde runs behave as they do without the flag:

```````````````````````````````` example
This ~text~~ is curious.
.
<p>This ~text~~ is curious.</p>
.
--fstrikethrough --fstrikethrough-double-only
````````````````````````````````

```````````````````````````````` example
foo ~~~bar~~~
.
<p>foo ~~~bar~~~</p>
.
--fstrikethrough --fstrikethrough-double-only
````````````````````````````````

The flag does nothing on its own, without `MD_FLAG_STRIKETHROUGH`:

```````````````````````````````` example
~~Hi~~ Hello, world!
.
<p>~~Hi~~ Hello, world!</p>
.
--fstrikethrough-double-only
````````````````````````````````

Single tildes claimed by `MD_FLAG_SUBSCRIPTS` are unaffected, because
subscripts are recognized before strike-through:

```````````````````````````````` example
H~2~O and ~~gone~~
.
<p>H<sub>2</sub>O and <del>gone</del></p>
.
--fstrikethrough --fstrikethrough-double-only --fsubscripts
````````````````````````````````
